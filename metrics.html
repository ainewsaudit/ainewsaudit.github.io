AI Detection Metrics Explained

Understanding how we measure AI-generated content in news articles

How does AI detection work?

We use Pangram, a state-of-the-art AI detector, which analyzes text on two levels:

  • Chunk-level: Breaks the article into chunks and analyzes each one for AI use.
  • Article-level: Uses those chunk-level results to give an overall judgment for the entire article.

Content Categories

👤

Human

Written entirely by humans

⚡

Mixed

Combination of human and AI content

🤖

AI

Generated primarily by AI

🤖

AI Likelihood

Overall article score

The overall probability that the entire article contains AI-generated content.

Range: 0% - 100%
Interpretation: Higher percentages indicate greater likelihood of AI generation
📊

Max AI Likelihood

Highest chunk score

This is the highest AI likelihood score found in any single chunk of the article. It's useful for spotting a "hidden" AI paragraph, even if the rest of the article looks human.

Range: 0% - 100%
Use: Identifies specific AI-generated sections within the article
📈

Average AI Likelihood

Average chunk score

The average AI likelihood score across all chunks of the article.

Range: 0% - 100%
Use: Shows overall AI content consistency across the article
⚡

AI Delta

Variation in chunk scores

The difference between the maximum and average AI likelihood scores. High delta means that some parts of the article have more AI content than others.

Range: 0% - 100%
High Delta: Mixed human/AI content
Low Delta: Consistent content (AI or human)
📊

AI Content Fraction

Percentage of AI-generated content

This estimates what percentage of the total text was written by AI. It's more specific than the other scores, trying to answer "how much" of the article is AI.

Range: 0.0% - 100.0%
Examples:
• 0% = Fully human-written
• 50% = Half AI, half human
• 100% = Fully AI-generated
