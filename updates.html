October 18, 2025 Launch Initial data

AI News Audit goes live

We have launched AI News Audit to help track the use and disclosure of AI in American newspapers. The first batch of data was collected roughly twice a week between June 15, 2025 and September 15, 2025. It includes 186,507 articles from online editions of 1,528 American newspapers published during that summer. The articles were tested with Pangram 2.0, which has a reported false positive rate of 0.001 for news articles. In this batch, about 9% of newly published news articles were detected as either partially or fully AI-generated.
