# AI News Audit

A comprehensive analysis platform that examines **251,000+ news articles** to detect and track AI-generated content across different media sources.

## 🔍 What This Site Does

This platform helps you understand the prevalence of AI-generated content in news media by analyzing articles from three major datasets:

- **Recent News**: 186,512 articles from various news sources
- **Opinions**: 44,803 opinion pieces and editorials  
- **Reporters**: 20,154 articles from reporter-specific sources

## 🚀 Key Features

### Advanced Search & Filtering
- **Smart Search**: Find articles by title, content, or author
- **AI Detection**: Filter by AI likelihood scores and predictions
- **Topic Analysis**: Browse by subject matter (Politics, Technology, Sports, etc.)
- **Date Filtering**: Search within specific time periods
- **Dataset Comparison**: Compare content across different media types

### Interactive Analytics
- **Prediction Distribution**: See the breakdown of Human vs AI vs Mixed content
- **Topic Trends**: Explore what topics are most prevalent
- **Time Series Analysis**: Track AI content trends over time
- **Clickable Charts**: Click on any chart segment to see specific examples

### Detailed Article Analysis
- **AI Likelihood Scores**: Multiple detection metrics for each article
- **Window Analysis**: See how AI detection varies across different parts of articles
- **Publication Info**: View circulation, ownership, and language data
- **Original Sources**: Access the full articles at their original publications

## 📊 Understanding the Data

### AI Detection Categories
- **Human**: Content written entirely by humans
- **Mixed**: Content with some AI-generated elements
- **AI**: Content primarily or entirely generated by AI

### Key Metrics
- **AI Likelihood**: Overall probability the article contains AI content
- **Max AI Likelihood**: Highest detection score from any part of the article
- **Average AI Likelihood**: Mean score across all analyzed segments
- **Fraction AI Content**: Percentage of the article estimated to be AI-generated

## 🔬 Technical Details

This is a **fully static website** that runs entirely in your browser:
- No data is sent to external servers
- All analysis happens locally on your device
- Data is compressed and cached for fast loading
- Works offline after initial load
